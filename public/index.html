MISSION BRIEF

Talk. Compare. Escape.

In co-op, the host is Engineering. The next two players become Communications and Navigation. All three must be connected to submit a stage.

Each station has a clue and an input. Your clue may solve somebody else’s input. First, use live power controls to diagnose a damaged feed; Engineering runs the system check. Later, set your answer and lock it. All three station locks submit a sequence.

Wrong answers clear the locks, not your choices. Earlier results remain in the mission log. Each puzzle has two hints. In multiplayer, all three players must vote before a new hint is revealed to the crew. You can withdraw your vote. Previous/Next revisits already revealed hints without another vote. There is no countdown and no penalty for exploring.

Online rooms use a server relay. Reload and rejoin with the same code in the same tab to reclaim your role. Rooms expire two hours after creation. All three players must be online to lock a sequence. The host can release offline seats. Direct P2P rooms are experimental: keep the host tab open; refreshing it ends the room.
