MISSION BRIEF

Talk. Compare. Escape.

Online rooms support one, two or three players across three stations. A new room starts with its creator controlling all stations. Joining players receive a station; use station tabs when you control more than one. The coordinator can assign stations in the crew panel.

The ten-level campaign starts with simple controls and introduces harder systems gradually. Read the current goal, compare your station’s information with your crew, and follow the check or lock instruction. Trace reads a relay; isolation shuts it off. They are different actions.

Wrong answers keep your choices. Earlier results stay in the mission log. Each level has two hints. In multiplayer, every assigned player must vote before a new hint is revealed. You can withdraw your vote, and Previous/Next revisits revealed hints. There is no countdown and no penalty for exploring.

Campaign rooms save progress for 24 hours from creation. Reload and rejoin using the same code in the same browser tab to reclaim your role. All assigned operators must be online to submit. If someone leaves, the coordinator can Continue with fewer players, preserving progress. Returning players do not automatically reclaim reassigned stations. Classic direct P2P is experimental and retains the original three-puzzle mission; keep its host tab open.
